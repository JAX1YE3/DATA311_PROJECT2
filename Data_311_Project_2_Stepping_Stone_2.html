Purpose

This document cleans and documents the two core Stage 1 datasets following the VDS process: understand the domain → examine the data → write action items → encode decisions in code. Raw files are never overwritten. Code is hidden; the readable product is the narrative plus the change / issue log.

Step 1 — Domain / observational units

  • Survey (simulated_commuter_survey_2020_26_messy.csv): one row = one respondent × one survey year.
  • Population (population_addresses_messy.csv): one row = one person (campus role + current and permanent address).
  • Third data source (not used yet): routing/transit data from HTA GTFS and Valhalla/OSM. The team read DATA311_Routing_Transit_Exploration_Colab.ipynb; derived measures will be pulled in later stages.

We followed the Synthetic Commuter Survey and Synthetic Campus Population data dictionaries from Canvas. Ambiguities we noticed:

  • pct_* columns should sum to 100, but the dictionary doesn’t say what to do with rows that don’t.
  • respondent_id repeats within a year with different answers, so it is not a unique key.
  • The population dictionary doesn’t say how to treat out-of-state ZIPs or states (permanent addresses legitimately vary; current ones should be local).
  • mode_primary is “mode with largest Q7 %”, but Walk is split across two columns; we combined them.
  • Hinarr Hu Moulik housing is only valid in 2025-26.

Step 5b — Nominatim address check (geocodability)

We followed the course Nominatim notebook: structured queries, a 1.1 s rate limit, an identifying User-Agent, and a cache. Only addresses that passed the Step 5 format check are sent. The full run was done once by the team and is not re-run on render (eval: false).

Status labels: ok (house number, road, and city agree), not_found, and review statuses (partial_match_review, mismatch_review, review). Rows that failed the format check are skipped_invalid_format, and rows not yet sent are not_checked. A match is evidence of geocodability, not USPS deliverability or proof the person lives there. Raw address columns will not be overwritten.

Step 6 — Save cleaned data beside the raw files

Wrote: survey_clean.csv, population_clean.csv, change_log.csv

assumptions

  • Numeric type conversions (pct_*, trips_per_week) and whitespace trimming are applied to every column but not logged cell by cell.
  • Invalid numbers (pct_*, days_commuting) are flagged, not guessed.
  • mode_primary is recomputed only for invalid labels; valid ones are only relabeled. Walk = on-campus + off-campus walking.
  • Blank trips_per_week is filled as 2 × days_commuting, per the dictionary, only when days is valid.

unresolved issues

  • Truncated/transposed ZIPs are flagged but not repaired.
  • Rows with pct_* not summing to 100 are kept
